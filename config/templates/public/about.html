About FoodShare

Turning Surplus Food Into Shared Value

FoodShare is a community-focused platform designed to connect people and organizations with surplus food to those who can benefit from it.

Food. People. Community.
Our Purpose

Building a More Connected Food Community

Every day, usable food can become surplus for households, restaurants, events, organizations and businesses.

FoodShare provides a digital platform where donors can share available food and receivers can discover and request suitable donations.

The goal is to make the donation process organized, transparent and easier to manage.

Our Mission

To provide a reliable digital platform that makes surplus food donation easier, faster and more organized.

Our Vision

To help build communities where edible surplus food is shared responsibly instead of being unnecessarily wasted.

Our Values

What Guides Us

Community

Connecting people through meaningful food-sharing opportunities.

Responsibility

Encouraging responsible handling and distribution of surplus food.

Sustainability

Supporting efforts to reduce unnecessary food waste and its environmental impact.
